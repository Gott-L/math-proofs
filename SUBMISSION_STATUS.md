Historical builds and axiom audits are not a declaration that the newly required `lean-verify` workflow has passed. Any future Lean submission must identify an exact complete proof commit and report an actual full self-check of that commit.

The separate full local E922 development is not offered here as a cleared public submission. Its inherited-source redistribution permissions, attribution and exact public verification target still need to be resolved.

No complete-problem acceptance, candidate registration, first-priority determination or award is claimed. Project roles are described in [README.md](README.md); original mathematical authors and prior formalizers retain their credits.
